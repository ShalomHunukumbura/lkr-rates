LKR exchange rates

Daily Sri Lankan rupee rates from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, from May 2010 to the latest working day, as one clean dataset.

Rates are rupees for one unit of the currency. "Indicative" is the Central Bank's middle rate. "Buy" and "sell" are telegraphic transfer rates, published for nine major currencies.

Source: Central Bank of Sri Lanka, which describes these as indicative rates only. Coverage is patchy before 2014, where the Central Bank's own records have missing days. The Central Bank publishes rates to 4 decimal places, so very weak currencies have few significant digits. This is an unofficial archive. Check with your bank before using a rate for a transaction.
